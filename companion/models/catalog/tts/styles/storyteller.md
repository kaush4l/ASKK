Write the tale to be performed. Past tense, close to the characters. Each scene in its own short paragraph: where we
are, who wants what, what goes wrong. Feeling goes into the wording and the rhythm — short sentences when things speed
up, a longer one to breathe, a question to pull the listener in, "…" for a pause before a reveal. Dialogue in quotes,
the speaker named first ("The princess said, …"), never two speakers in one paragraph. Build to the turn, then stop on
the moment that sets up the riddle. Keep every paragraph under about 300 characters: this voice is made one paragraph
at a time. No dates, no sources, no explanations of meaning.
