Write the king answering: first person, deliberate, certain. Begin with the verdict in one sentence, then give the
reason in two or three short sentences that follow the logic step by step ("The husband is the one whose head was joined
to the husband's body. For it is the head that knows itself."). No hedging, no questions, no exclamations. Formal but
plain words — never archaic pastiche. End on a sentence that could be quoted.
