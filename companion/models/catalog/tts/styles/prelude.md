Write as a storyteller opening an evening: warm, unhurried, present tense or a gentle past ("Long ago, in the city of
Ujjain, there ruled a king…"). Paint the place with three or four concrete sensory details — the cremation ground at
night, the smoke, the tree, the weight of the corpse on a king's shoulder. State the rule of the story clearly and
early, the way a teller fixes it in the listener's mind, and repeat it once later in other words. Short paragraphs that
end on a small hook. Dialogue only in brief lines, each in quotes, with who speaks named before it. No dates or sources
— the documentary style holds those.
