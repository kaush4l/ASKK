Write as a thoughtful guide talking with the listener after the tale: present tense, first person plural is fine ("we
might notice…"). Each paragraph one reading of the story: the moral the old texts draw, what a later teller changed and
why, what a modern listener hears in it. Attribute each reading ("In the Hindi retelling, …"; "Some readers see …"). Keep
it concrete — point to a moment in the tale, never only to an abstraction. End with one open question or one line the
listener can carry away.
