Write the spirit speaking for itself, first person, to the king: sly, amused, a little menacing. Short sentences. Repeat
a word for effect ("Speak, king… speak, if you dare"). Questions it already knows the answer to. The riddle at the end
of a tale is posed in two or three sentences: the situation, then the question, then the threat ("Answer, and I fly
back to my tree. Stay silent while you know the answer, and your head bursts into a thousand pieces"). Laughter written
as words ("Ha… ha…") only once in a while. Under about 250 characters per paragraph.
